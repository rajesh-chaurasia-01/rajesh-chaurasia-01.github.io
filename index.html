Qulacs RetinaVision AI

Eye disease detection

Hybrid quantum plus classical screening for cataract, glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, and AMD.

RetinaVision analyzes retinal fundus photographs as a clinician-reviewed decision-support system. Classical image processing handles raw fundus quality, vessel, disc, macula, contrast, and lesion cues, while Qulacs-backed quantum circuits transform features and support decision making for faster, more robust early detection.

Qulacs Quantum Engine Hybrid ML Fundus Imaging Clinical Decision Support

Decision support only: final diagnosis, treatment, and referral remain clinician-led.

Fusion pipeline

  • Fundus image intakeAccepts standard retinal photographs from common clinical capture workflows.
  • Classical feature extractionProcesses contrast, optic disc, macula, vessels, blur, drusen, exudates, and lesion indicators.
  • Quantum feature transformUses quantum circuits for feature mapping and decision support through the Qulacs engine.
  • Clinical outputReturns ranked differential diagnosis, confidence, risk level, flags, recommended investigations, and follow-up timeline.

Cataract

Lens haze, reduced contrast, color shift, and image clarity patterns for early screening.

Glaucoma

Cup-to-disc ratio, RNFL thickness, optic disc asymmetry, and high-risk structural cues.

Diabetic Retinopathy

Microaneurysms, exudates, vessel tortuosity, and neovascularization indicators.

AMD

Drusen, retinal pigment epithelium changes, macular damage, and neovascularization signals.
