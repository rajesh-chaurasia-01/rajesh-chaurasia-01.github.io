Developed a Hybrid Quantum Model combining Classical Feature Extraction with Quantum Image Classification for cataract identification. The project leverages quantum ML concepts including feature maps, amplitude encoding, angle encoding, discrete time quantum walks, and swap tests to measure image overlap between test and reference samples. Achieves superior pattern recognition in medical eye images — demonstrating real quantum advantage in healthcare diagnostics.

Built a Quantum Random Number Generator leveraging quantum superposition and entanglement to produce truly random numbers for cryptographic applications. Integrated QRNG into RSA key generation to produce unpredictable keys resistant to pattern-based attacks.
